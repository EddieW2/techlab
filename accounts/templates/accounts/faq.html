Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

🛍️ How do I place an order?

1. Browse products by category or use the search bar.
2. Click a product to view its details.
3. Select a quantity and click “Add to Cart.”
4. Go to your cart and click “Checkout.”

👤 Do I need an account to order?

No, you can login as a guest to make a quick purchase, but if you wish to save your cart and view your receipt you must make an account.

🔐 How do I reset my password?

Click “Forgot Password” on the login page, and you’ll receive an email with a link to reset it.

💳 What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Visa, MasterCard, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

🚚 How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days. You'll get a tracking number once your order ships.

💬 How can I contact customer support?

📦 Can I track my order?

Yes! After shipping, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also check your order status in your account.

NO REFUNDS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

🧾 Where can I view my receipts?

Click the Receipts icon in the navigation bar to see your recent purchases and total amounts.

❤️ How does the wishlist work?

Click the heart icon on any product to save it. Access your wishlist via the heart icon in the navbar.
